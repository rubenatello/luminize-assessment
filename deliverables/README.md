# Submission deliverables

## 1. Data model

- **Assessment brief, pages 1–2:** BigQuery Bronze/Silver/Gold layers, schema, grains, keys, and ongoing data-quality operating model.
- **Leadership deck, slide 3:** one-page medallion diagram.
- Supporting detail: [data model](../docs/data_model.md), [identity-resolution framework](../docs/identity_resolution_and_confidence.md), [schema-drift and replay controls](../docs/schema_drift_replay_and_validation.md), and [BigQuery SQL](../sql/bigquery/).

## 2. Profitability analysis

- **Profitability workbook:** contribution margin by brand and SKU, inventory health, data-quality register, assumptions, and reconciliation checks.
- **Assessment brief, pages 3–4:** calculation definition, results, assumptions, and top three leadership recommendations.
- **Leadership deck, slides 2 and 4–8:** decision narrative and supporting evidence.

## 3. First-90-day automation proposal

- **Assessment brief, page 5:** one-page sequence and two implementation paths.
- **Leadership deck, slide 9:** efficient/cost-aware versus premium/managed choice.
- Supporting detail: [90-day proposal](../docs/automation_90_day_plan.md) and [architecture options](../docs/architecture_options.md).

The presentation is designed for approximately 20–25 minutes, leaving the balance of a 45–60 minute session for discussion.
